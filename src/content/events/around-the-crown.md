**Around the Crown 10K** is our next race as a team, and it's one of the most
iconic runs in Charlotte. Once a year, the city closes down the **I-277 inner
loop** — the "crown" that wraps Uptown — and lets runners take over the highway
for a fast, flat 10K through the heart of the city.

The race starts and finishes at **Truist Field**, home of the Charlotte Knights,
right in Uptown. At **6.2 miles** it's a great next step whether you're moving up
from a 5K or chasing a 10K personal best, and the closed-highway course means
wide-open lanes and skyline views the whole way.

## Join the Stride City team

Stride City Run Club is running Around the Crown together, and we'd love for you
to line up with us. Register through the official race page, then watch our
Wednesday runs and socials for team meetup details as race day gets closer.

## What to expect

- **Distance:** 10K (6.2 miles) on Charlotte's I-277 loop
- **When:** Sunday, September 6, 2026 at 7:30 AM
- **Where:** Start and finish at Truist Field, Uptown Charlotte
- **After the race:** A finish festival with medals and refreshments, plus the
  official after-party at NoDa Brewing

Around the Crown sells out, so if you're planning to run with us, grab your spot
early. All paces are welcome — come to race, run, or cheer the team on.
