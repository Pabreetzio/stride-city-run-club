Stride City Run Club put together a team for **Run the South Half Marathon, 10K &
5K**, one of Charlotte's summer race events at **McAlpine Creek Park**. Known as
**"The Hottest Half,"** the race included multiple distance options across a
two-day format, including a Friday evening 5K and Saturday morning 10K and Half
Marathon.

The event took place at **McAlpine Creek Park, 8711 Monroe Rd, Charlotte, NC
28212**. The schedule listed the **5K for Friday, June 19, 2026 at 6:15 PM**,
followed by the **Half Marathon on Saturday, June 20, 2026 at 7:00 AM** and the
**10K on Saturday, June 20, 2026 at 7:30 AM**.

Run the South was a great fit for Stride City because the course is described as
fast and flat, with a route through mostly shaded greenways and wide open gravel
paths. Whether chasing a PR, trying a new distance, or just looking for a fun
race-day atmosphere with familiar faces, it was a strong summer goal race for the
club.

All distances included race swag such as running sunglasses, a shirt option, and
a custom finisher medal. The race also offered virtual options for runners who
wanted to participate on their own schedule.

## Race options

- **5K** — Friday, June 19 at 6:15 PM
- **10K** — Saturday, June 20 at 7:30 AM
- **Half Marathon** — Saturday, June 20 at 7:00 AM
- **Challenge options** — Run the Friday 5K, then come back Saturday for the 10K or Half Marathon

## How it went

Run the South was a chance to show up together, celebrate the progress we've made
on Wednesday nights, and bring that same Stride City energy to the starting line.
