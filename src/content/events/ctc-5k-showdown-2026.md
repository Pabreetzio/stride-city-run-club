Start Saturday with the **Charlotte Track Club 5K Showdown**, a 5K built around friendly competition between local run clubs. Whether you're chasing a fast finish or joining the team atmosphere, individual runners are welcome too.

## Team challenge

- Teams need **at least five and at most fifteen runners**.
- Each team's **top five finishers** score points based on finishing place, not finishing time. The lowest total wins.
- Sixth-place finishers are used to break ties; additional runners may displace other teams' scorers.
- Clubs may enter multiple teams.

If you'd like to represent **Stride City Run Club**, coordinate with other members so there are enough runners for a team. Team lineup and meetup details haven't been announced here.

## Race morning

- **Where:** Frank Liske Park, 4001 Stough Rd, Concord, NC 28027
- **Packet pickup:** 7:00–8:00 AM at the park. Pickup closes at 8:00 AM.
- **Start:** 9:00 AM sharp. The start area closes after runners take off.
- **Distance:** 5K (3.1 miles).

[Register for the CTC 5K Showdown](https://www.simplyregister.net/register/?e=129676) or [read the organizer's full race details](https://ctc5kshowdown.godaddysites.com/).

**Double-race Saturday:** There's another Stride City calendar event later today: the [Out of the Ashes 5K & Remembrance Walk](/events/out-of-the-ashes-2026), with an evening 5K start at 5:00 PM.
