Stride City Run Club showed up together for **Run Jen Run**, a Charlotte 5K at
**Symphony Park** benefiting the **Go Jen Go Foundation**, which funds patient
assistance and family support for local breast cancer patients. It's exactly the
kind of race we love — a fast, friendly morning that gives back to the community
we run through every week.

The **5K kicked off at 8:00 AM**, followed by a **Fun Run at 8:45 AM** and an
awards ceremony at 9:15. Beyond the race itself, the morning was a full community
event: a Fun Zone for families, beverage stations, and a photo booth, all in a
headphone-free environment built for connecting with the runners around you.

## A race with a cause

Every registration helped Go Jen Go support survivors, thrivers, and fighters —
survivors ran free in 2026 — and the first 500 5K finishers took home the year's
finisher medal.

## Stride City on the day

We came out to run, walk, and cheer as a team, and to celebrate the progress
we've built on our Wednesday night runs. Run Jen Run is a great reminder of why
we lace up together: to move, to support one another, and to show up for our
neighbors.
